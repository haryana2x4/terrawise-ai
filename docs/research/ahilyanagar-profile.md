# District Agro-Climatic Profile: Ahilya Nagar (formerly Ahmednagar)
*Also referenced as Ahilyanagar / Ahmednagar in historical and state databases*

**State:** Maharashtra, India  
**Geographical Coordinates:** 18°20' N to 19°59' N Latitude, 73°40' E to 75°43' E Longitude  
**Total Geographical Area:** 17,048 sq. km (Largest district in Maharashtra)  
**Headquarters:** Ahilya Nagar (Ahmednagar)  
**Key Research Institutions:** Mahatma Phule Krishi Vidyapeeth (MPKV), Rahuri; Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) Babhaleshwar (Rahata), KVK Dahigaon Ne (Shevgaon).

---

## 1. Talukas and Administrative Division
The district comprises **14 Talukas** distributed across three revenue sub-divisions:
1. **Northern / Canal-Irrigated Talukas:** Rahuri, Shrirampur, Kopargaon, Rahata, Sangamner, Nevasa.
2. **Western Hilly / Heavy Rainfall Taluka:** Akole (Western Ghats catchment).
3. **Southern & Eastern Scarcity / Drought-Prone Talukas:** Ahilya Nagar (Nagar), Parner, Karjat, Jamkhed, Shevgaon, Pathardi, Shrigonda.

---

## 2. Agro-Climatic Zones
Ahilya Nagar spans three distinct National Agricultural Research Project (NARP) agro-climatic zones:
1. **Scarcity Zone (MH-6):** Covers >70% of the district (Parner, Karjat, Jamkhed, Pathardi, Shevgaon, Shrigonda, Nagar). Annual rainfall 500–600 mm, high coefficient of variation (35–45%), chronic dry spells in July–August.
2. **Western Maharashtra Plain Zone (MH-5):** Covers central talukas (Rahuri, Shrirampur, Nevasa, Rahata, Kopargaon). Rainfall 600–750 mm, canal command areas of Pravara and Mula systems.
3. **Western Ghat / Sub-Mountain Zone (MH-3):** Covers Akole taluka. Rainfall 1,500–2,500 mm, hilly terrain, high runoff.

---

## 3. Rainfall Normals and Variability
- **Normal Annual Rainfall:** 578.8 mm (IMD District Normal, 1981–2010 baseline).
- **Monsoon Rainfall (June–Sept):** ~440 mm (76% of total).
- **Post-Monsoon (Oct–Nov):** ~95 mm (16% of total).
- **Variability:** 
  - Akole receives >1,800 mm annually.
  - Karjat and Jamkhed receive <480 mm in deficit years.
  - Frequent mid-season dry spells lasting 18 to 28 days during July and August.

---

## 4. Temperature Ranges
- **Summer (March–May):** Min 22°C, Max 41.5°C (Extreme heatwave peaks reach 43.5°C in April–May in Shevgaon and Nevasa).
- **Kharif (June–Oct):** Min 21°C, Max 33°C.
- **Rabi (Nov–Feb):** Min 8.5°C, Max 30°C. (Cold wave episodes in December–January drop night temperatures to 6.5°C in Rahuri/Sangamner).

---

## 5. Major Soil Types and Properties
- **Deep Black Soils (Vertisols):** Prevalent in river basins of Godavari, Pravara, and Mula (Kopargaon, Rahata, Shrirampur, Nevasa). Clay content 45–60%, high water holding capacity (180–220 mm/m), high montmorillonite swelling-shrinking clay, prone to waterlogging and salinity under over-irrigation.
- **Medium Black Soils:** Predominant in Sangamner, Rahuri, Nagar. Depth 30–60 cm, well-drained, suitable for pulses, soybean, and bajra.
- **Coarse Shallow Soils (Entisols / Inceptisols):** Common on undulating plateaus of Parner, Pathardi, Karjat. Depth <25 cm, low organic carbon (<0.35%), acute moisture stress during 10-day dry spells.

---

## 6. Irrigation Infrastructure and Water Resources
- **Major Reservoirs:**
  - **Bhandardara Dam (Wilson Dam)** on Pravara river (Akole).
  - **Mula Dam** on Mula river (Rahuri) — capacity ~26 TMC.
  - **Nilwande Dam (Upper Pravara)** — caters to drought-prone Sangamner, Rahata, Kopargaon.
- **Canal Networks:** Pravara Left & Right Bank Canals, Mula Canals, Godavari Left & Right Bank Canals, Kukadi Canal System (supplying Parner, Shrigonda, Karjat).
- **Groundwater Status (CGWB Report 2023):**
  - **Over-exploited talukas:** Sangamner, Kopargaon, Rahata (stage of groundwater extraction >100%).
  - **Critical / Semi-critical:** Parner, Shrigonda, Rahuri.
  - **Safe:** Akole.

---

## 7. Cropping Systems and Seasonal Patterns
1. **Kharif Season (June to October):**
   - **Bajra (Pearl Millet):** Predominant in drought-prone eastern talukas.
   - **Soybean:** Widespread in Rahuri, Shrirampur, Nevasa.
   - **Cotton:** Southern talukas (Shevgaon, Pathardi, Nevasa).
   - **Kharif Onion:** Critical cash crop in Sangamner, Parner, Rahuri.
   - **Pulses (Tur, Moong, Urad):** Intercropped with bajra and cotton.
2. **Rabi Season (October to March):**
   - **Rabi Sorghum (Jowar):** Maldandi 35-1 staple across scarcity zone.
   - **Gram (Chickpea / Harbhara):** High adoption under residual moisture (Digvijay, Vijay varieties from MPKV).
   - **Wheat:** Irrigated zones along Mula-Pravara canals.
   - **Late Kharif / Rangada Onion:** Transplanted in Sept–Oct, harvested in Dec–Jan.
3. **Summer Season (March to June):**
   - **Summer Groundnut & Bajra:** Restricted to assured borewell/canal zones.
   - **Summer Onion (Pol / Rangi):** Major storage crop.
4. **Perennial Horticulture & Cash Crops:**
   - **Sugarcane:** Central belt along sugar cooperative belts.
   - **Pomegranate (Bhagwa variety):** Sangamner, Rahata, Rahuri.
   - **Guava (Sardar / L-49):** Rahata and Kopargaon.

---

## 8. Agricultural Markets (APMC Mandis)
- **Lasalgaon APMC (Extended trading zone):** Asia's largest onion market hub touching northern boundary.
- **Rahuri APMC:** Regional hub for onion, gram, and jowar.
- **Shrirampur APMC:** Sugarcane jaggery, soybean, and maize.
- **Ahilya Nagar (Nagar) APMC:** Central terminal market for pulses, bajra, and livestock.
- **Sangamner APMC:** Major tomato and pomegranate terminal.

---

## 9. Priority Climate Risks and Frequency
- **Mid-Monsoon Dry Spells (July/August):** 4 out of 5 years in Parner, Karjat, Jamkhed.
- **Unseasonal Hailstorms (Feb–March):** Occurs 1 in 3 years; devastating to rabi wheat, gram, and horticulture.
- **Thermal Stress (>40°C during March):** Forces premature forced-maturity in rabi wheat (terminal heat stress).
- **Waterlogging / Salinity:** In low-lying canal command Vertisols in Shrirampur and Nevasa.
