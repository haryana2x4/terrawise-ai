# Comprehensive Pest and Disease Calendar: Ahilya Nagar & Nashik
*Integrated Pest Management (IPM) First Protocol*

**Priority Focus Crops:** Onion, Grapes, Pomegranate, Tomato, Sugarcane, Soybean, Bajra, Jowar, Maize, Wheat, Bengal Gram, Pigeon Pea, Cotton, Groundnut, Green Gram, Black Gram.  
**Official Sources:** MPKV Rahuri Package of Practices, ICAR-NRC Grapes Pune, ICAR-NRC Pomegranate Solapur, Maharashtra Agriculture Department (CROPSAP Surveillance), NCIPM New Delhi.

---

## 1. Safety Protocols and Mandatory Advisory Rules

> [!CAUTION]
> **MANDATORY SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS FOR FARMERS:**
> 1. **IPM First:** Always deploy cultural, mechanical, and bio-control methods before any chemical spray.
> 2. **Official Dosage Only:** Chemical recommendations must strictly comply with CIB&RC (Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee) registered labels and MPKV Rahuri recommendations. Where an official formulation is not verified, this system displays **"Ask KVK / कृषी विज्ञान केंद्राचा सल्ला घ्या"**.
> 3. **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Always wear protective mask, rubber gloves, goggles, and long-sleeved clothing during spraying.
> 4. **Weather Caution:** Never spray in strong winds (>12 km/h) or within 3 hours of impending rainfall.
> 5. **Water Protection:** Keep spray equipment and washings at least 50 meters away from wells, borewells, farm ponds, canals, and livestock drinking troughs.
> 6. **Waiting Period (PHI):** Strictly observe the Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI) indicated on the product container before harvesting any edible produce.

## 2. Crop-by-Crop Pest & Disease Directory

### 1. Onion (कांदा / Allium cepa)

#### A. Onion Thrips (*Thrips tabaci*)
- **Favorable Weather:** Dry weather, temperatures between 28°C–34°C, relative humidity < 60%, absence of rain for >10 days.
- **Vulnerable Stage:** Seedling to vegetative bulb development (30 to 75 days).
- **Symptoms:** Silvery white streaks or patches on inner leaf folds, leaf curling and crinkling, stunted bulb formation.
- **IPM Cultural & Biological Controls:**
  - Install yellow and blue sticky traps (25 traps per hectare) at canopy height.
  - Intercrop 2 rows of maize or barrier rows of sorghum around the onion plot as windbreak and thrips barrier.
  - Foliar spray of entomopathogenic fungus *Verticillium lecanii* (5 g/litre of water) or Neem seed kernel extract (NSKE 5%) during early infestation.
- **When to Call KVK:** When count exceeds economic threshold level (ETL) of >15–20 thrips per plant.
- **Chemical Option (MPKV Official):** Fipronil 5% SC @ 1.0 ml/litre OR Lambda-cyhalothrin 5% EC @ 0.6 ml/litre with sticker (Sandovit @ 0.5 ml/L). Observe 15-day PHI.

#### B. Purple Blotch (*Alternaria porri*) & Stemphylium Blight
- **Favorable Weather:** High relative humidity (>80%), intermittent rainfall/drizzles, temperatures between 22°C–28°C.
- **Vulnerable Stage:** Bulb initiation and vegetative expansion (50 to 90 days).
- **Symptoms:** Small, water-soaked lesions that turn brown-purple with concentric rings; leaves break at the lesion point.
- **IPM Cultural & Biological Controls:**
  - Crop rotation of at least 2 years without allium crops.
  - Seedling root dip in *Trichoderma harzianum* formulation (5 g/L water) for 15 minutes before transplanting.
  - Avoid excess nitrogenous fertilizer; ensure adequate drainage.
- **When to Call KVK:** If purple lesions appear on more than 5% of leaf surface.
- **Chemical Option (MPKV Official):** Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/litre OR Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.5 ml/litre.

### 2. Grapes (द्राक्षे / Vitis vinifera)

#### A. Downy Mildew (*Plasmopara viticola*)
- **Favorable Weather:** 10-10-10 Rule (Temperature >= 10°C, >= 10 mm rainfall/wetness, shoots >= 10 cm long); Relative humidity > 85%, continuous leaf wetness > 4 hours.
- **Vulnerable Stage:** Post-October fruit pruning: sprout, flowering, and young pea-sized berry stage (15 to 60 days).
- **Symptoms:** Oily yellow "oil spots" on upper leaf surface with white downy growth on the underside; infected blossom bunches dry out and turn brown.
- **IPM Cultural & Biological Controls:**
  - Canopy management: timely shoot thinning and leaf defoliation to maximize air circulation and sunlight penetration.
  - Prophylactic spray of *Trichoderma asperellum* or *Bacillus subtilis* (2 g/L) before flowering.
- **When to Call KVK / NRC Grapes:** Immediately when oil spots appear on leaf canopy or inflorescences.
- **Chemical Option (NRC Grapes / CIB&RC):** Prophylactic: Bordeaux mixture (1%) or Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L. Curative: Dimethomorph 50% WP @ 1.0 g/L OR Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.0 g/L. Strictly observe export MRL and PHI guidelines.

#### B. Powdery Mildew (*Erysiphe necator*)
- **Favorable Weather:** Moderate temperatures (20°C–28°C), cloudy shade, low to moderate humidity (40–70%), dry canopy surface.
- **Vulnerable Stage:** Berry softening, veraison, and canopy development.
- **Symptoms:** Ashy-gray powdery patches on leaves, tendrils, and berries; berry cracking and skin hardening.
- **IPM Controls:** Dust sulfur (300 mesh) early morning when dew is present; spray wettable sulfur 80% WDG @ 2.0 g/litre.
- **When to Call KVK:** When berry splitting or powdery patches breach 2% of bunches.

### 3. Pomegranate (डाळिंब / Punica granatum)

#### A. Bacterial Blight / Telya (*Xanthomonas axonopodis pv. punicae*)
- **Favorable Weather:** Cloudy weather with intermittent warm rains, temperatures 25°C–35°C, high humidity (>70%), splash dissemination during windstorms.
- **Vulnerable Stage:** All stages from flowering to fruit bulking.
- **Symptoms:** Oily, water-soaked triangular black lesions on leaves; severe dark-brown cracked "L" or "Y" shaped lesions on fruit rind; fruit splitting.
- **IPM Cultural & Biological Controls:**
  - Strict orchard sanitation: prune affected twigs with sterilized secateurs (dip in 2% sodium hypochlorite after every cut).
  - Paste cut ends with Bordeaux paste (10%).
  - Avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation; maintain clean ground under tree canopy.
- **When to Call KVK / NRCP:** At first sign of oily black spots on leaves or fruit twigs.
- **Chemical Option (NRCP Solapur Official):** Spray Streptocycline @ 0.5 g/L mixed with Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L with non-ionic sticker. Repeat after 12 days in wet spells.

### 4. Tomato (टोमॅटो / Solanum lycopersicum)

#### A. Early Blight & Late Blight (*Alternaria solani* & *Phytophthora infestans*)
- **Favorable Weather:** Temperatures 18°C–24°C, persistent dew/fog, relative humidity > 90%.
- **Vulnerable Stage:** Vegetative to fruit maturity (35 to 80 days).
- **Symptoms:** Target-board concentric brown rings (Early Blight); water-soaked rotting lesions on leaves and green fruit with white mold under damp conditions (Late Blight).
- **IPM Controls:** Staking and raised bed cultivation; mulching with silver-black reflective plastic; spray *Trichoderma* enriched FYM.
- **Official Formulation:** Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.0 g/litre OR Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/litre.

#### B. Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV) & Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*)
- **Favorable Weather:** Dry, hot weather (28°C–35°C) favoring whitefly vector multiplication.
- **Symptoms:** Upward leaf curling, thickening, severe stunting, and bushy rosette habit.
- **IPM Controls:** Erect yellow sticky cards (30/ha); net nurseries with 40-mesh insect proof nylon netting; spray Neem oil 10,000 ppm @ 2 ml/L.
- **Chemical:** Ask KVK.

### 5. Soybean (सोयाबीन / Glycine max)

#### A. Stem Fly (*Melanagromyza sojae*) & Girdle Beetle (*Obereopsis brevis*)
- **Favorable Weather:** Warm humid monsoon weather, intermittent rains, temperature 26°C–32°C.
- **Vulnerable Stage:** Seedling to vegetative flowering (15 to 45 days).
- **Symptoms:** Plants show wilting of terminal shoots; reddish-brown gall or girdled rings on stem nodes; hollowed stem pith.
- **IPM Controls:** Seed treatment with Thiamethoxam 30% FS @ 10 ml/kg seed; clip and destroy girdled branches.
- **Chemical (MPKV):** Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml/L or Ask KVK.

#### B. Rust (*Phakopsora pachyrhizi*)
- **Favorable Weather:** Continuous drizzle, temperature 20°C–25°C, relative humidity > 80% for >3 days.
- **Symptoms:** Brown pustules on lower leaf surface, premature leaf drop.
- **Official Formulation:** Hexaconazole 5% EC @ 1.0 ml/L or Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1.0 ml/L.

### 6. Bengal Gram / Chickpea (हरभरा / Cicer arietinum)

#### A. Gram Pod Borer (*Helicoverpa armigera*)
- **Favorable Weather:** Clear sunny days following light rain, temperature 20°C–28°C during flowering.
- **Vulnerable Stage:** Flowering and pod formation (45 to 80 days).
- **Symptoms:** Ragged foliage, bored round holes in green pods with caterpillar body half inside.
- **IPM Controls:**
  - Install bird perches (T-shaped bamboo sticks, 50/ha) for predatory birds.
  - Install pheromone traps (Helilure @ 10/ha) for adult moth monitoring.
  - Spray HaNPV (Helicoverpa Nuclear Polyhedrosis Virus) @ 250 LE/ha in evening hours with 0.1% jaggery.
- **Chemical (MPKV Official):** Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L OR Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 ml/L.

#### B. Fusarium Wilt (*Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. ciceris*)
- **Favorable Weather:** Soil temperature > 25°C with fluctuating subsoil moisture.
- **Symptoms:** Drooping of petioles and leaflets, dark internal vascular browning when root is split lengthwise.
- **IPM Controls:** Seed treatment with *Trichoderma viride* @ 5 g/kg seed + Rhizobium @ 25 g/kg seed; use resistant varieties (Digvijay, Phule Vikram, Vijay).

### 7. Cotton (कापूस / Gossypium hirsutum)

#### A. Pink Bollworm (*Pectinophora gossypiella*)
- **Favorable Weather:** Warm, overcast weather during boll maturation; temperatures 24°C–32°C.
- **Vulnerable Stage:** 60 to 120 days (squaring, flowering, boll formation).
- **Symptoms:** Rosette flowers (petals twisted closed like a rosette); bored bolls showing exit holes; stained lint and eaten seeds.
- **IPM Controls:**
  - Install Pheromone traps (Gossyplure @ 10/ha) at 45 days.
  - Release egg parasitoid *Trichogramma bactrae* @ 1.5 lakh eggs/ha at weekly intervals.
  - Strict termination of crop by December to break seasonal pest cycle.
- **Chemical:** If ETL exceeds (8 moths/trap/night for 3 consecutive nights), consult KVK immediately.

### 8. Maize & Sorghum (मका आणि ज्वारी)

#### A. Fall Armyworm (*Spodoptera frugiperda*) on Maize
- **Favorable Weather:** Temperatures 24°C–32°C, dry spells interspersed with light rainfall.
- **Vulnerable Stage:** Knee-high to tasseling (15 to 55 days).
- **Symptoms:** Papery windows on young whorl leaves; massive saw-dust like fecal frass inside the central whorl; ragged leaf margins.
- **IPM Controls:** Hand-pick egg masses; apply neem cake in whorls; whorl application of dry sand mixed with lime (9:1) to mechanically disrupt caterpillar feeding.
- **Biological / Chemical:** Spray *Metarhizium rileyi* (2 g/L) or Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 0.5 ml/L.

#### B. Sorghum Shootfly (*Atherigona soccata*) on Rabi Jowar
- **Favorable Weather:** Late sowing (post-October 15), high relative humidity.
- **Symptoms:** Dead heart (drying of central seedling shoot) within 4 weeks of emergence; rotten smell upon pulling dead shoot.
- **IPM Controls:** Sow timely before October 15; increase seed rate by 20% and rogue out infested dead hearts; seed treatment with Imidacloprid 70% WS @ 10 g/kg seed.
