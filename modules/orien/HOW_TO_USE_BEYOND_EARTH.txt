## 3. All Interactive Controls & Enderman Powers
1. **Uncaged Newtonian Physics**:
   - Click and drag the Enderman anywhere, release to fling him.
   - Real gravity, floor bounce, purple dust particles, and fall damage indicators.

2. **Carrying Minecraft Blocks**:
   - Press **[B]** or click **📦 Block** to toggle and cycle through 4 block types:
     - Grass Block
     - TNT Block
     - Ender Chest
     - Cosmic Core

3. **Ender Pearl Throwing**:
   - Press **[E]** or click **🔮 Pearl**:
   - Flings an Ender Pearl in a ballistic arc with trailing sparks.
   - On impact, triggers a teleport boom and teleports the Enderman there!

4. **Eye-Contact Stare Rage**:
   - Hover your cursor over the Enderman's eyes for > 1.1s, or press **[S]** / click **⚠️ Stare**:
   - Lower jaw unhinges wide, eye sclera turns blazing red, screen shakes, and Ender roar sounds.

5. **Petting Reaction**:
   - Scrub the mouse back and forth across his head, or press **[P]** / click **❤️ Pet**:
   - Ascending pixel hearts float into the air, and he emits cute purr chirps and heals health.

6. **Beatbox & Dance**:
   - Press **[M]** or click **🎵 Beat**:
   - Plays a procedural 8-bit beatbox beat while the Enderman grooves with articulating jaw lip-sync.

7. **Aura Customization**:
   - Click **🌈 Aura** to cycle between Void Purple, Cosmic Cyan, Solar Gold, and Rainbow Prism.

8. **Rocket Jump**:
   - Press **[Space]** or click **🚀 Fling** to launch him into zero-gravity.

9. **Ender Dragon Flyby**:
   - Press **[D]** or click **🐉 Dragon** in the top bar or dock:
   - The Minecraft Ender Dragon swoops across the sky with articulated wings and breath particles.

10. **Conversational Buddy Brain**:
    - Ask questions about NASA SMAP, ECOSTRESS, crop rotation, climate shifts, or witty everyday topics.
    - Full speech audio + 60 FPS phonetic lower jaw lip-sync in 20+ languages!
